About Mykonos

Born in Indonesia, built for the world. The scent of a slow life.

Our Story

Mykonos began as a small operation in Indonesia, initially releasing a single men's cologne and a single women's perfume. Within two years, the portfolio grew rapidly, driven by online demand and a commitment to skin-safe, long-lasting fragrances.

The brand's name draws on the Greek island of Mykonos — a nod to the breezy, sun-kissed vibe the founders wanted to capture in each bottle. Today, Mykonos is expanding across Southeast Asia and beyond, with branches in Malaysia and the Philippines, and a recent entry into the United States market.
