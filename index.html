The Scent of a Slow Life

Mykonos began as a small operation in Indonesia, releasing a single men's cologne and a single women's perfume. Within two years, the portfolio grew rapidly — driven by online demand and a commitment to skin-safe, long-lasting fragrances.

The name draws on the Greek island of Mykonos — a nod to the breezy, sun-kissed vibe captured in each bottle. Today, Mykonos expands across Southeast Asia and beyond, with branches in Malaysia and the Philippines, and a recent entry into the United States market.
